I spent the afternoon thinking about the scene in the novel where the brother leaves without explaining himself. I don't care whether that scene is realistic; I care that I kept defending him in my head as if he were someone I owed. I texted Lina to say the book was getting under my skin, then deleted the message instead of sending it. Later I underlined one sentence in chapter seven and wrote "this is the part I keep living" in the margin. I still can't tell whether I miss Arun or only miss the version of myself that existed around him.
