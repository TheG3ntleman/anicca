Anicca /ə-ˈnɪ-t͡ʃə/ n.
  1. Impermanence; the transient, ever-changing nature of phenomena.
  2. In Buddhism, one of the three marks of existence (with dukkha and anattā), pointing to the inevitability of change.

From Pāli a- “not” + nicca “constant, lasting.”
